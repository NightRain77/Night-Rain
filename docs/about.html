About Night Rain Creations

How did I get started? - I've helped my mom with her gardening for years, and I kept thinking it was such a shame that the beautiful flowers she grew only lasted a short time. I wanted to find a way to preserve their beauty and share it with others, so I began experimenting with pressed flowers. I liked the idea of putting the flowers on jars because jars have so many potential uses! Over time I have branched out and now I do a lot more different types of glassware, and create some smaller pieces such as magnets, keychains, necklaces, and phone cases as well.

Is this your full-time job? No, its just a hobby! When I started doing this I was working full-time. I am currently working part-time, and getting a lot of Saturday hours, which is why my attendance at events has been lower this year.

Can you actually drink out of the glasses? The resin I use is FDA approved for direct contact with food. I only coat the OUTSIDE of the glassware, so the only contact would be with your mouth. I have personally used my drinking glasses without issue, and have never received any complaints from coustomers. If the resin worries you, using a straw to avoid contact with the resin is an option. But also, glassware is super versatile so you don't have to use it for drinking just because it looks like a drinking glass!

Tips for Caring for Your Glassware:

  • Hand wash only. Do not put in dishwasher.
  • Do not soak in water for long periods of time.
  • Do not use abrasive cleaners or scrubbers.
  • Do not use any harsh chemicals or solvents.
  • Do not microwave. Resin only tolerates low to moderate heat, up to 135-158 F.
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to preserve color.
